## The Legal Compliance Challenge

### What You Need to Comply With

**UK GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation):**
- Lawful basis for data processing
- Privacy by design
- Data retention policies
- Right to be forgotten
- Data breach notification (72 hours)
- Privacy Policy requirements

**PECR (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations):**
- Email marketing consent
- SMS marketing consent
- Cookie consent requirements
- Opt-out mechanisms

**Accessibility Requirements:**
- WCAG 2.1 AA standards
- Accessibility Statement
- Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations 2018 (may apply)

**Payment Processing:**
- PCI DSS compliance (via Stripe/PayPal)
- Secure payment data handling
- Refund policy requirements

**Consumer Rights:**
- Consumer Rights Act 2015
- Distance Selling Regulations
- Cancellation rights

### Your Specific Situation

**As Plugin Developer (You):**
- Process customer booking data
- Store personal information (names, emails, phone numbers)
- Send transactional emails (confirmations, reminders)
- Send marketing emails (newsletters, promotions)
- Future: SMS reminders
- Payment processing (Stripe/PayPal)
- Google Calendar integration (data sharing)

**As Agency (Serving Clients):**
- Clients use your plugin for their customers
- You manage hosting (data processor role)
- You may access client data for support
- Clients are data controllers
- You need clear data processing agreements

### Penalties for Non-Compliance

**GDPR Violations:**
- Tier 1: Up to €10 million or 2% of global turnover
- Tier 2: Up to €20 million or 4% of global turnover
- Examples: £183 million (British Airways), £20 million (Marriott)

**PECR Violations:**
- Up to £500,000 per violation (ICO can impose)
- Marketing without consent: £50,000-500,000

**Accessibility Violations:**
- Legal action under Equality Act 2010
- Compensation to affected users
- Reputational damage

**Why You Can't Ignore This:**
- Even small businesses get fined
- One data breach = business-ending costs
- Clients demand proof of compliance
- Professional obligation
