## CONTEXT

`MySchedule.vue` renders booking cards in three sections (today, week, upcoming). Extension Vue apps need a stable DOM attribute on each card to identify which booking it represents — for example, to inject a meeting-link badge. Vue's `:key` binding is internal and does not render to the DOM as an attribute. This task adds `:data-booking-id="booking.id"` (or the correct alias) to the outer `<div>` of every booking card in all three `v-for` loops. Template-only change — no logic, no styling, no computed properties touched.
