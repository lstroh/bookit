## Executive Summary

### The Challenge

As a WordPress booking plugin agency, you need to manage:
- **Lead capture** from website, networking, referrals
- **Sales process** from inquiry to signed contract
- **Client onboarding** questionnaires, information gathering
- **Project delivery** website development, plugin setup, training
- **Ongoing relationships** maintenance invoices, support requests, upsells
- **Documentation** proposals, contracts, invoices, client communications

**Without a system:** Manual tracking in spreadsheets, lost emails, forgotten follow-ups, inconsistent client experience, time wasted chasing clients for information.

**With a proper system:** Automated workflows, professional client experience, nothing falls through cracks, more time for development, scalable process.

### The Solution

Implement a client management platform that handles the complete lifecycle:

```
Lead → Qualification → Proposal → Contract → Payment → 
Onboarding → Delivery → Invoice → Ongoing Maintenance
```

### Software Options Summary

| Platform | Best For | Price | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|----------|----------|-------|-----------|------------|
| **Bonsai** | Starting out (0-10 clients) | £17/mo | All-in-one, affordable, web design focus | No client portal, basic PM |
| **HoneyBook** | Growing (5-30 clients) | £30/mo | Automation, beautiful UX, service business focus | No time tracking |
| **ClientManager** | Established (10+ clients) | £99/mo | Built for web designers, unlimited clients | Higher cost for solo start |
| **Pipedrive + Tools** | Sales-focused agencies | £15/user + tools | Best CRM, integrations | Requires multiple tools |
| **HubSpot Free** | Budget start | Free | Free CRM, marketing tools | Limited features, expensive upgrades |

### My Recommendation

**Phase 1 (Months 1-6, First 5 Clients):**
- Start with **Bonsai (£17/month)** or **HubSpot Free + Google Forms (£0)**
- Learn the process, keep costs low
- Focus on delivery, not tools

**Phase 2 (Months 7-12, 5-15 Clients):**
- Upgrade to **HoneyBook (£30/month)**
- Automation saves time at scale
- Professional client experience
- Worth the investment when you have recurring revenue

**Phase 3 (Year 2+, 15+ Clients):**
- Consider **ClientManager (£99/month)** if web design specific features needed
- OR stay with HoneyBook (it scales well)
- Add specialized tools as needed (Pipedrive for sales, Content Snare for questionnaires)

### Stage 1: Lead Generation

**Goal:** Capture leads from multiple sources into one system

#### Lead Sources

**Your Website:**
- Contact form: "Get a Free Consultation"
- Service page: "Request a Quote"
- Blog posts: "Download Free Guide" (lead magnet)
- Pricing page: "See Pricing for Your Business"

**Networking:**
- Business networking events (Chamber of Commerce, BNI)
- LinkedIn connections
- Referrals from existing clients
- Industry meetups (salon/therapy/photography associations)

**Outreach:**
- Cold emails to target prospects
- LinkedIn messages
- Follow-up from networking events

#### What Happens

**With CRM Software:**
```
Lead fills out website form
    ↓
Automatically added to CRM (HoneyBook/Bonsai/HubSpot)
    ↓
You receive notification (email/mobile app)
    ↓
Lead tagged by source (website, referral, networking)
    ↓
Automatic welcome email sent to lead
    ↓
Task created for you: "Follow up with [Name] within 24 hours"
```

**Without Software (Manual):**
```
Lead fills out website form
    ↓
Email lands in inbox
    ↓
You manually add to spreadsheet
    ↓
You manually send reply email
    ↓
You manually set reminder to follow up
    ↓
(High risk of forgetting or delayed response)
```

#### Information Captured

**Minimum (Contact Form):**
- Name
- Email
- Phone number
- Business type (salon, therapist, photographer, etc.)
- How did you hear about us?
- Brief message about their needs

**Ideal (Qualification Form):**
- All of the above, PLUS:
- Business name
- Website URL (if existing)
- Number of staff
- Average bookings per day
- Current booking system (if any)
- Budget range
- Timeline (When do you need this?)
- Biggest challenge with current system

#### CRM Actions

**Lead Status:** New Lead → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal Sent → Won/Lost

**Automatic Emails:**
```
Email 1 (Immediate): "Thank you for your inquiry"
Email 2 (24 hours later, if no reply): "Following up on your inquiry"
Email 3 (7 days later, if no reply): "Last chance - would you like to discuss?"
```

**Manual Actions:**
- Review lead details
- Research their business (website, social media, reviews)
- Tag as "High Priority" (fits ideal client profile) or "Low Priority"
- Schedule discovery call or send additional info

### Stage 2: Initial Contact & Qualification

**Goal:** Determine if prospect is a good fit, book discovery call

#### Qualification Criteria

**Ideal Client Profile:**
- UK-based service business
- 1-10 staff members
- Takes appointments/bookings
- Budget: £500-1,500 setup + £30-200/month ongoing
- Ready to start within 3 months
- Currently using manual bookings or outdated system

**Red Flags (Not a Good Fit):**
- Budget under £300 total
- Needs custom features beyond booking (eCommerce, membership site, etc.)
- Expects website in 1 week
- Unwilling to pay monthly for hosting/maintenance
- Vague about needs ("I just want a website")

#### First Contact Email Template

**Subject:** Re: Your booking system inquiry - [Business Name]

**Body:**
```

Thanks for reaching out about a booking system for [Business Name]!

with a professional WordPress website and custom booking system.

Based on what you shared, I think we'd be a great fit. Here's what I'd 
suggest as a next step:

Let's schedule a 20-minute call where I can learn more about your business
and walk you through how the booking system works.

You can book a time that suits you here: [Calendly link]

Or if you prefer, just reply with a few times that work for you.

Looking forward to chatting!

Best,
Liron
[Your Agency Name]
[Phone] | [Website]
```

#### Qualification Call (10-15 minutes)

**Script Outline:**

1. **Introduction (2 min)**
   - "Thanks for taking the time to chat. Tell me a bit about your business..."

2. **Understand Current Situation (3 min)**
   - "How are you currently managing bookings?"
   - "What frustrates you most about your current system?"
   - "How many staff members take bookings?"
   - "About how many bookings do you handle per day?"

3. **Assess Needs (3 min)**
   - "Do you currently have a website?" (if yes: get URL)
   - "What's most important to you: easier staff scheduling, more online bookings, better customer experience?"
   - "Are you looking for just the booking system, or a complete website redesign?"

4. **Budget & Timeline (2 min)**
   - "Have you thought about budget for this project?"
   - "When are you hoping to have this up and running?"

5. **Next Steps (2 min)**
   - "Great! Based on what you've shared, I think we can definitely help."
   - "The next step is a more detailed discovery call (30 min) where I'll ask specific questions about your services, staff, and booking flow."
   - "After that, I'll put together a custom proposal with exact pricing and timeline."
   - "Does [date/time] work for a discovery call?"

#### CRM Updates

**After Qualification Call:**
- Update lead status: "Qualified" or "Not a Fit"
- Add notes from call (budget, timeline, pain points)
- Tag by business type (salon, therapist, photographer)
- Tag by package (Regular or Premium)
- Schedule discovery call (if qualified)
- Set reminder: "Prepare for discovery call with [Name]" (day before)

**If Not a Fit:**
- Update status: "Lost - Not a Fit"
- Add reason (budget too low, needs features we don't offer, etc.)
- Send polite decline email with referrals to other solutions

### Stage 3: Discovery Call

**Goal:** Gather all information needed to create accurate proposal

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes

**Format:** Video call (Zoom, Google Meet) - more personal than phone

#### Pre-Call Preparation

**Research:**
- Review their website (if exists)
- Check social media (Facebook, Instagram)
- Read Google/Yelp reviews (understand customer pain points)
- Note competitors' websites (what they might want to emulate)

**Documents Ready:**
- Notepad/CRM to capture answers
- Sample websites to show (if needed)
- Pricing calculator (to estimate on the fly)

#### Discovery Call Outline

**1. Introduction & Agenda (5 min)**
```
"Hi [Name], great to see you! Thanks for making time today.

Here's what I'd like to cover in the next 30 minutes:
1. Learn about your business and services (10 min)
2. Understand your booking process and pain points (10 min)
3. Walk through our solution and how it would work for you (5 min)
4. Discuss budget, timeline, and next steps (5 min)

Sound good? Any questions before we dive in?"
```

**2. Business Overview (10 min)**

*Questions to ask:*
- "Tell me about your business - what services do you offer?"
- "How long have you been in business?"
- "How many staff members do you have? Will they all use the booking system?"
- "Who handles bookings currently? (Owner, receptionist, each staff member?)"
- "Do staff need business email addresses, or can they use personal?"
- "What makes your business different from competitors?"

*Capture:*
- Services offered (list of 5-10 services with typical duration)
- Staff count (for email accounts and hosting decision)
- Business hours
- Unique selling points (for website copy)

**3. Current Booking Process & Pain Points (10 min)**

*Questions to ask:*
- "Walk me through what happens when a customer wants to book an appointment today."
- "Where do most bookings come from? (Phone, Facebook, walk-ins?)"
- "Do you take payments upfront, deposits, or pay on the day?"
- "How do you handle cancellations and reschedules?"
- "What's the most frustrating part of your current system?"
- "How often do you have no-shows? Would reminders help?"

*Capture:*
- Current booking method (phone, paper diary, basic online form)
- Payment timing (none, deposit, full payment)
- Biggest pain points (double bookings, no-shows, time wasted on phone)
- Desired features (SMS reminders = Phase 2 upsell opportunity)

**4. Website & Branding (5 min)**

*Questions to ask:*
- "Do you have an existing website?" (if yes: get URL, ask what they like/dislike)
- "Do you have a logo and brand colors?" (if yes: request files)
- "Are there any websites you really like the look of?" (competitors or other industries)
- "What do you want customers to feel when they visit your website?"

*Capture:*
- Website redesign or new website
- Branding assets available (logo, colors, photos)
- Design inspiration (URLs of websites they like)
- Tone/style preference (professional, friendly, luxury, etc.)

**5. Technical Requirements (5 min)**

*Questions from Hosting Strategy Document:*
- "How many staff will be using the dashboard daily?" (hosting decision)
- "How many bookings do you handle per day?" (hosting decision)
- "Do you plan to sell products on the website?" (eCommerce = Premium hosting)
- "Do you want Google Calendar integration?" (already planned, but confirm)

*Capture:*
- Regular or Premium hosting needed (based on decision matrix)
- Email accounts needed (owner + reception only, or all staff?)
- Additional features (eCommerce, blog, photo gallery)

**6. Budget & Timeline (5 min)**

*Questions to ask:*
- "Have you set aside a budget for this project?" (pause and let them answer)
- "For context, our packages range from £495-995 for setup, plus £30-200/month for hosting and maintenance. Does that fit your budget?"
- "When would you ideally like to launch?" (gives you timeline)
- "Is there a specific event or season you're aiming for?" (salon may want it before Christmas rush)

*Capture:*
- Budget confirmed (Regular £644 or Premium £1,290)
- Timeline (ASAP, 1 month, 3 months, flexible)
- Hard deadline (if any)

**7. Solution Overview (5 min)**

*What to present:*
```
"Great! Based on everything you've shared, here's what I recommend:

1. **Website:** Modern, mobile-friendly design showcasing your [services]
2. **Booking System:** Customers book online 24/7, choose their service, 
   staff member, date, and time
3. **Staff Dashboard:** You and your team see all bookings in one place, 
   get reminders, sync to personal Google Calendar
4. **Payments:** Optional deposit or full payment via Stripe/PayPal
5. **Email Notifications:** Automatic confirmations and reminders sent 
   to customers
6. **Hosting & Maintenance:** We handle everything technical, you focus 
   on your business

Based on your [X staff and Y bookings/day], I'd recommend our 
[Regular/Premium] package."
```

*Show:*
- Demo booking flow (screen share your demo site)
- Sample dashboard screenshots
- Example confirmation emails

**8. Next Steps (3 min)**

*Closing:*
```
"Perfect! Here's what happens next:

1. I'll put together a custom proposal for you with exact pricing, 
   timeline, and what's included
2. You'll receive that within 2 business days
3. We can schedule a quick call to review it together, or you can 
   review on your own and let me know if you have questions
4. If everything looks good, you can sign the contract and make the 
   first payment right from the proposal (it's all digital, very easy)
5. Once that's done, we'll send you a detailed questionnaire to 
   gather all the info we need to start building

Sound good? Any questions for me?"
```

*Schedule:*
- Proposal review call (optional, 15 min)
- Expected start date (if they sign this week)

#### Post-Call CRM Updates

**Immediately after call:**

### Stage 5: Contract & Payment

**Goal:** Get legally binding agreement and first payment with minimal friction

#### Contract Essentials

**What MUST be in the contract:**

1. **Parties**
   - Your business name and details
   - Client business name and details

2. **Scope of Work**
   - Specific deliverables (website design, booking system setup, etc.)
   - What's included vs. out of scope
   - Number of revision rounds

3. **Timeline**
   - Project start date
   - Estimated completion date
   - Milestones (design approval, content due, launch)

4. **Payment Terms**
   - Setup fee: £X (50% deposit, 50% on completion)
   - Monthly fees: £X/month
   - Payment method (bank transfer, card)
   - Late payment terms (interest after 30 days)

5. **Ownership & Rights**
   - Client owns final website and content
   - You retain right to showcase in portfolio
   - Third-party licenses (WordPress plugins, themes)

6. **Responsibilities**
   - Client provides: Content (text, photos), feedback within [X days], payment on time
   - You provide: Design, development, training, support

7. **Cancellation & Refunds**
   - If client cancels before work starts: Full refund minus £50 admin fee
   - If client cancels during project: Deposit non-refundable, balance prorated
   - Monthly services: 30 days notice to cancel

8. **Warranty & Support**
   - 30-day bug fix warranty after launch
   - Ongoing support included in monthly maintenance
   - Client responsible for own content updates

9. **Limitation of Liability**
   - You're not liable for client's lost revenue due to website downtime
   - Total liability limited to fees paid

10. **Governing Law**
    - UK law applies
    - Jurisdiction: [Your location] courts

### Platform-Specific Deep Dives

#### HoneyBook - The All-in-One for Service Businesses

**Website:** https://www.honeybook.com  
**Best For:** Growing agencies (5-30 clients), service businesses wanting professional client experience  
**Pricing:** £30/month (billed annually) or £39/month (monthly)

**Pros:**
- ✅ **Beautiful client experience** - Templates are stunning, clients love the professional look
- ✅ **Automation heaven** - Set up workflows once, they run forever (saves hours)
- ✅ **All-in-one** - Replaces 5-10 separate tools
- ✅ **Easy to use** - Intuitive interface, minimal learning curve
- ✅ **Service business focused** - Built for creatives, consultants, agencies
- ✅ **Mobile app** - Manage clients on the go
- ✅ **Payment processing** - Stripe & PayPal built-in

**Cons:**
- ❌ **No time tracking** - Need separate tool (Toggl, Harvest)
- ❌ **No free tier** - Must pay from day one
- ❌ **Limited project management** - Basic task lists, not full PM
- ❌ **Expensive for solo start** - £30/month when you have 1-2 clients feels high

**Workflow in HoneyBook:**

*Lead Capture:*
```
1. Embed HoneyBook form on your website ("Get Started")
2. Lead fills out form → Automatically added to HoneyBook CRM
3. You receive notification (email + mobile app)
4. Lead receives automated welcome email (template you create once)
```

*Proposal & Contract:*
```
1. Create proposal from template (5 minutes)
   - Auto-fills client info from CRM
   - Drag-and-drop pricing sections
   - Add images, testimonials, FAQ
2. Attach contract template
3. Set payment schedule (50% deposit, 50% on completion)
4. Send to client (one click)
5. Client reviews, signs, pays - all in one flow
6. You receive notification: "Client signed & paid!"
```

*Onboarding:*
```
1. Client pays deposit → Triggers automated workflow
2. Onboarding questionnaire automatically sent
3. Client fills out → You receive notification
4. Welcome packet automatically sent (PDF guide, links, etc.)
5. Kickoff call automatically scheduled (via integrated Calendly)
```

*Project Management:*
```
1. Create project from template
2. Task list: Design, Development, Testing, Launch
3. Assign due dates
4. Client sees progress in their portal
5. Upload files for client review
6. Client approves/requests changes (comments)
```

*Invoicing:*
```
1. Create invoice from template
2. Recurring invoice setup (monthly maintenance)
3. Automatic sending (1st of each month)
4. Automatic payment (saved card)
5. Automatic receipt
```

**Templates Included:**
- Proposals (web design, branding, consulting)
- Contracts (service agreements, terms)
- Questionnaires (discovery, onboarding)
- Invoices (one-time, recurring)
- Email sequences (welcome, follow-up, reminder)

**HoneyBook Recommendation:**
✅ **Start here if:** You have 5+ clients OR budget for tools (£30/month affordable)
❌ **Skip if:** Solo start with 0-2 clients (too expensive for beginning)

**Cons:**
- ❌ **Limited free features** - No proposals, no contracts, no payments
- ❌ **Contact limits** - Free tier caps marketing contacts (charges above limit)
- ❌ **Expensive paid tiers** - £45/month (Starter), £360/month (Professional)
- ❌ **Complex** - Lots of features, overwhelming for beginners

**Free Tier Includes:**
- Contact management (unlimited contacts)
- Email tracking (see when opened)
- Email templates
- Meeting scheduler
- Forms (for website)
- Live chat
- Basic reporting

**What's NOT Free:**

- Marketing automation (need paid tier)
- Advanced reporting (need paid tier)
