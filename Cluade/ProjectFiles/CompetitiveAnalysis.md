## PART 1: WORDPRESS BOOKING PLUGINS

### **1. BOOKLY** 

**Pricing Model:**
- Bookly is available on a perpetual license at $89
- Free version available with basic features
- Bookly Pro includes customer support and lifetime updates
- Add-ons sold separately (major cost concern)

**Key Features:**
- Unlimited staff members with individual working schedules, unlimited services grouped into categories, Google Calendar 2-way sync
- Email and SMS notifications, importable/exportable customer base
- Payment integrations: PayPal, Stripe, 2Checkout, Authorize.Net, Mollie, etc.
- Package bookings, recurring appointments, group bookings (via add-ons)

**User Reviews - What People LOVE:**
- Easy to set up without coding experience
- Clean,  user-friendly interface
- Straightforward setup - even WordPress beginners can handle it
- Good Google Calendar synchronization
- Visual, color-coded calendar

**User Reviews - MAJOR PAIN POINTS:**
- Users mention frequent bugs when using multiple add-ons and feel the extra costs for features are excessive
- Updates can disrupt functionality
- Licensing issues - codes registered to wrong domains, support doesn't resolve problems
- One user reported constant downtime for scheduled maintenance, features regularly deleting customer profiles and bookings
- Cannot use on staging servers with different domains - forces local installation for testing
- Users describe updates breaking existing functionality as something customers shouldn't have to pay to fix

**Critical Insight - Add-On Cost Trap:**
The free/basic version is very limited. To get features your personas need (packages, recurring appointments, deposits, group booking, custom duration), you must buy multiple add-ons at additional cost each. This "nickel and dime" approach frustrates users.

**Technical Approach:**
- WordPress plugin (requires WP admin access for configuration)
- All management done through WordPress backend
- No separate business dashboard

### **2. AMELIA**

**Pricing Model:**
- Two types of licenses: annual subscription and lifetime purchase
- Free Lite version available with limited features
- Plans: Starter, Standard, Pro, Elite (per-site licensing)
- The Starter plan doesn't include basic tools like location management, customer notifications, or paying with a deposit

**Key Features:**
- Recurring appointments (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly), packages of appointments with discounted pricing, multiple service durations with different prices
- Employee Panel and Customer Panel - let employees and customers manage bookings without WordPress Dashboard access, straight from frontend
- Native Zoom integration, special days support, 2-way Google Calendar integration
- Event booking (classes, conferences, multi-day events)
- Resources management (shared equipment, rooms, etc.)

**User Reviews - What People LOVE:**
- Users appreciate clean, modern interface, easy for both clients and admins
- Automated appointment management with reminders and notifications to avoid no-shows
- Seamless integration with Google Calendar and Outlook
- Support team described as excellent, with one developer switching from Bookly to Amelia specifically for better support

**User Reviews - PAIN POINTS:**
- Complex pricing structure with many features varying by plan
- Basic tools like invoices and taxes are locked in the cheapest plan
- Per-site licensing (expensive for web developers building multiple client sites)
- WooCommerce required for many payment gateways (not included in lower tiers)

**Critical Differentiator vs. Bookly:**
Amelia offers Employee Panel and Customer Panel where users can manage bookings without WordPress access - this partially addresses your custom dashboard vision, though it's still within WordPress ecosystem.

**Technical Approach:**
- WordPress plugin with frontend panels
- Employees/customers access via shortcodes on front-end pages
- Still tied to WordPress, but less WP admin exposure

## PART 2: SaaS BOOKING PLATFORMS

### **3. FRESHA (Major UK competitor for salons/beauty)**

**Pricing Model:**
- Basic plan at $19.95 per month for solo practitioners, Plus plan at $9.95 per team member monthly
- Automated appointment reminders with included text messaging allowance, additional SMS charged pay-as-you-go after allowance
- **CRITICAL**: 20% commission (minimum $6) when new clients book through their marketplace
- Payment processing fees (around 2.6% on transactions)

**Key Features:**
- Unlimited bookings, locations, team members, notifications
- Online booking through Google search, Facebook, Instagram pages, and website integration
- Mobile apps for both business and clients
- Built-in POS system
- Marketing campaigns (email/SMS)
- Smart pricing (off-peak discounts, last-minute offers)

**User Reviews - What People LOVE:**
- Users say the online booking system saves time and improves professionalism for both staff and clients
- Very easy to use - clients love the booking experience
- Fresha regularly adds new features based on community feedback
- Mobile app highly rated (4.9 rating)

**User Reviews - MAJOR PAIN POINTS:**
- Users charged the 20% new client fee even for existing clients who scan in-store QR codes or use direct booking links
- Support team described as dismissive, curt, with no empathy - felt like talking to a bot programmed to say no
- Customers must create Fresha accounts to book - adds friction
- Cannot customize booking page design to reflect branding
- Limited integrations - locked into Fresha ecosystem
- Hidden/changing fees - started free, now has subscriptions + marketplace fees

**Critical Business Model Issue:**
The 20% marketplace commission is highly controversial. Users report being charged this fee unfairly, even for their own clients, and support refuses refunds.

## **4. CALENDLY** (Major SaaS Platform - General Appointments)

**Pricing Model:**
- Free plan, Standard at $12/seat/month, Teams at $16/seat/month (annual billing), Enterprise starting at $15,000/year
- Per-user pricing scales costs linearly with team size - a 10-person team on Teams plan would pay $1,920 annually
- All paid plans billed per user/per month

**Key Features:**
- Unlimited event types, pool availability for round-robin team assignment, group events, automated email reminders
- Time zone detection (highly praised feature)
- Integration with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, HubSpot
- Teams plan includes lead routing, round-robin scheduling, CRM integrations, advanced admin features
- Calendar syncing (Google Calendar, Outlook, Office 365)

**User Reviews - What People LOVE:**
- Automatic time zone detection critical for teams, eliminates confusion with time conversions
- Eliminates back-and-forth emails, allowing sharing of links for clients to book based on real-time availability
- Simple, clean interface
- Easy setup for basic scheduling needs

**User Reviews - MAJOR PAIN POINTS:**
- By default does NOT notify you of session bookings - user was half hour late for first client, must manually create workflow for notifications
- Calendar sync problems causing conflicting or double bookings, numerous patient complaints in optometry practice
- Lack of ability to accurately sync to Google calendar for PTO, vacation, or other blocks
- Unauthorized billing - user charged extra $15/month for seat added without authorization, only refunded $90 of $180
- No telephone support - customers spend over an hour on chatbox with unresolved issues
- Not focused on recurring appointments - more for one-off meetings which applies to multiple event types
- Free plan extremely limited (1 event type only)
- Support team very unresponsive on free plan, takes more than 5 days to reply to complaint tickets

**Critical Issues:**
- **NOT designed for service businesses with recurring appointments** (yoga classes, weekly therapy sessions, etc.)
- Designed for business meetings, not bookings
- Per-seat pricing becomes very expensive for teams
- Calendar sync reliability issues are a recurring complaint

**Target Market:**
- Business professionals scheduling meetings (sales calls, consultations)
- NOT ideal for service businesses (salons, therapists, etc.)

## **5. ACUITY SCHEDULING** (SaaS Platform - Now owned by Squarespace)

**Pricing Model:**
- Emerging plan at $16/month (annual), Growing at $27/month (annual), Powerhouse at $49/month (annual), plus Enterprise (custom pricing)
- Monthly billing available: Emerging $16, Growing $27, Powerhouse $49
- 7-day free trial (no ongoing free plan)
- 20% discount when choosing annual billing

**Key Features:**
- Emerging allows one calendar, Growing allows up to six calendars, Powerhouse allows 36 calendars, Enterprise unlimited
- Emerging supports email reminders only; other plans include SMS reminders
- Payment processing (Square, PayPal, Stripe)
- Custom intake forms
- Time zone conversion
- Packages, memberships, subscriptions
- Google Calendar, Outlook sync

**User Reviews - What People LOVE:**
- Customization options, payment integrations, ability to automate reminders make managing schedules easier
- Clean booking interface for clients
- Flexible and intuitive online booking
- Good for beauty/wellness businesses

**User Reviews - PAIN POINTS:**
- Pricing can be confusing or costly when advanced features are needed, learning curve for setup and customization
- Minimal features for relatively high price - only offers scheduling, payment processing and marketing must be purchased separately
- Features locked by tier - SMS reminders and advanced customization only in higher plans
- Per-user pricing adds up as teams scale and calendars are added
- Website creation via Squarespace is separate cost

**Critical Insight:**
Acuity is solid for scheduling but lacks all-in-one functionality. Many users feel they're paying premium prices for what should be basic features. The Squarespace integration means it's tightly coupled to that ecosystem.

**Target Market:**
- Service professionals (beauty, wellness, fitness, consultants)
- Better suited than Calendly for recurring appointments and service businesses

## **6. SIMPLYBOOK.ME** (SaaS Platform - Budget-Friendly)

**Pricing Model:**
- Free (50 bookings + 1 custom feature), Basic £7.50/month (100 bookings), Standard £20.90/month (500 bookings), Premium £41.90/month (2000 bookings)
- Pricing based on booking volume, not team size - Free handles 50 monthly bookings, Basic 100, Standard 500, Premium 2000
- **Unique pricing model**: Pay based on appointment volume, NOT per-user
- Custom features are add-ons (you choose which ones you need)
- SMS and custom domain charged separately

**Key Features:**
- Over 70 custom features available to reflect needs and style
- Unlimited clients on all plans
- Facebook booking widget
- Staff scheduling
- Automatic reminder emails
- Time zone conversion
- Google Calendar, Outlook, Microsoft Teams integration
- POS integration, memberships, coupons, gift cards

**User Reviews - What People LOVE:**
- Free service without time limit allows simple booking site when not earning yet, lots of features for free
- Customer service answers fast whether you pay or use free version, always helpful
- Very affordable compared to competitors
- Good for salons and small businesses

**User Reviews - PAIN POINTS:**
- Custom features limited on lower tiers - unlimited tier jump in price not worth it for just 1-2 more features
- Customer service responsive but not solution-oriented, admin interface far from intuitive and current UX standards
- Paid features not thought-through - group booking feature allows one person to book tour for others but doesn't work properly
- Booking volume limits can be restrictive - solo practitioner with heavy volume might need higher tier
- Learning curve for setup (many customization options can overwhelm non-designers)

**Critical Differentiator:**
Volume-based pricing instead of per-user is unique. Great for solo practitioners with high booking volume, but can feel limiting if you need many custom features.

**Target Market:**
- Budget-conscious small businesses
- Solo practitioners or micro-teams (1-3 people)
- Salons, therapists, tour guides, rental businesses

## **7. WOOCOMMERCE BOOKINGS ECOSYSTEM** (WordPress Plugins)

**Note:** There are multiple WooCommerce booking plugins, not just one official option.

### **Official WooCommerce Bookings**
**Pricing:**
- Premium extension starting at $249/year
- Requires active WooCommerce store

**Key Features:**
- Flexible booking duration, time zone support, confirmations and cancellations, automated email notifications, buffer/padding time, advanced pricing rules
- One-way sync with Google Calendar, smooth integration with other WooCommerce extensions

**Pain Points:**
- Priced at $249/year making it one of more expensive solutions, lack of depth in features might be limiting for complex needs
- Challenging learning curve despite extensive features
- Requires WooCommerce (adds complexity if client doesn't need e-commerce)

### **Third-Party WooCommerce Booking Plugins**
Multiple alternatives exist:
- **PluginHive Bookings & Appointments**: $149/year including 1 year updates and support
- **Booknetic**: Basic $79 one-time + $29 for WooCommerce integration, Premium $349 one-time with WooCommerce integration included
- **Bookly Pro**: Starting at $49/year

**Common Features Across WooCommerce Plugins:**
- Staff/resource management
- Dynamic pricing
- Calendar sync (Google, Outlook)
- Payment gateway integration
- Email/SMS notifications
- Booking management dashboard

**Major Limitation:**
All require WooCommerce installation, which adds:
- E-commerce complexity for non-selling businesses
- Additional plugin overhead
- Steeper learning curve
- WordPress admin access required for management
