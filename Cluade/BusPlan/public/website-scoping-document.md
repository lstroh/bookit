## What This Document Is

The Website Scoping Document is the internal bridge between the discovery call and the build. It is produced after the contract is signed and the client questionnaire is returned. It records every design and content decision made for this specific client's website — what pages, what components, what design direction, and why.

It has two outputs:

1. **This internal document** — detailed, research-backed, used by Liron to brief the build and make decisions. Not shown to the client.
2. **The Client Design Presentation** — a simplified, visual version of the design direction section, presented to the client for approval before build begins. Produced from Section 5 of this document.

---

## How to Use This Document

1. Create a copy of this template for each new client
2. Name it: `[ClientSlug]_website-scoping.md` (e.g. `bloom-salon_website-scoping.md`)
3. Store it in the client's Google Drive folder under `/[ClientName]/02-Scoping/`
4. Complete sections in order — do not skip ahead
5. When Section 5 is complete, produce the Client Design Presentation from it
6. When all sections are approved, use Section 7 as the build brief
