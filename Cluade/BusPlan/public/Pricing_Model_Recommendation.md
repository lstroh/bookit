## EXPECTED CUSTOMER OBJECTIONS & RESPONSES

### Objection 1: "That's more expensive than Bookly (£89)"

**Response:**
> "You're right—Bookly is £89. But Bookly is just a plugin. You still need to build the website yourself, figure out how to configure it, and train your team to use WordPress admin. We build everything for you. Your staff log into a professional dashboard that takes 5 minutes to learn, not WordPress. You're comparing apples to oranges—we're a complete solution, not a DIY tool."

**Follow-up:** "How much is your time worth? Most clients spend 20-30 hours setting up Bookly. At £25/hour, that's £500-750 before you even start."

---

### Objection 2: "Why monthly fees instead of one-time?"

**Response:**
> "Monthly fees mean you get ongoing value: we handle all software updates, security patches, and bug fixes. When Stripe changes their API—and they do—we update the plugin. When WordPress releases a new version, we test compatibility. Plus, you get support whenever you need it. One-time payment plugins stop development after the sale—you're on your own."

**Follow-up:** "Think of it like insurance—you're paying for peace of mind that everything keeps working."

---

### Objection 3: "Fresha is free, why would I pay?"

**Response:**
> "Fresha is free until you look at the fine print. They take 20% of every new client booking. If you're bringing in £200/week in new clients, that's £40/week—over £2,000 a year—going to Fresha. With us, you keep 100% of your revenue. After 18 months, we're actually cheaper."

**Follow-up:** "Plus, your customers don't have to create a Fresha account to book. And you own your website and data—you're not locked into their platform."

---

### Objection 4: "Can you lower the setup fee?"

**Response:**
> "The setup fee covers the time we invest building your custom website and configuring everything for your business. We could lower it, but we'd need to reduce what's included. Instead, consider the annual plan—it drops the setup fee to £495 and saves you £500 overall."

**Follow-up:** "We're confident enough to offer month-to-month after the setup. You're not locked in. If it doesn't work out, you can cancel anytime."

---

### Objection 5: "I need to think about it"

**Response:**
> "Of course—it's an important decision. While you're thinking, consider this: every week without online booking is lost revenue. Most of our clients see their first booking within 48 hours of going live. What's one lost booking worth to you?"

**Follow-up:** "Would it help if I showed you a demo of the dashboard your team would use?"
