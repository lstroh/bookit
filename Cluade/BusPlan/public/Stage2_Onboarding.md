## Stage 2 Overview

Stage 2 starts the moment a client says yes — verbally, by email, or by replying to the proposal. It ends when you have a signed contract, first payment confirmed, and a complete brief in hand. Nothing gets built until all three are done.

This stage is about momentum and professionalism. The client has just made a decision. Your job is to make the next steps feel easy, clear, and well-organised — so their confidence in choosing you is immediately reinforced.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Client confirms they want to proceed — verbally or by email |
| **Goal** | Signed contract, Invoice 1 paid, complete brief and assets received |
| **Duration** | Typically 5–10 working days (depends on client asset response speed) |
| **Tools** | Bonsai (contract, invoice), Stripe (payment), Google Drive (assets, brief) |
| **Outcome** | Internal project brief confirmed — Stage 3 (Build) begins |

## Step 2.1 — Send Contract

### Timing

Send the contract the **same day** the client confirms. Not the next morning. The moment they say yes, the contract goes out. This signals that you are organised and ready, and it keeps momentum alive before they have time to second-guess.

If they confirm by email late in the evening, it is acceptable to send the following morning — but not later than that.

### How to Send

Use Bonsai's built-in contract feature. The client receives a link, reviews the contract in their browser, and signs electronically. No printing, no scanning, no PDF back-and-forth.

Save a copy of the signed contract to the client's Google Drive folder under `Contracts/` immediately on signing.

### What the Contract Must Cover

Have a UK-qualified solicitor review your contract template before you use it with your first client. This is a one-off cost — worth it. The template below covers the essential clauses.

**Scope and delivery:**
- Specific deliverables — what is being built (reference the proposal)
- What is explicitly out of scope — content writing, logo design, custom features not listed
- Client asset delivery window — 5 working days from contract signing
- Revision policy — unlimited rounds within the original agreed scope; anything outside scope is quoted separately
- Estimated duration — 6 weeks from brief confirmed and Invoice 1 paid

**Payment:**
- Milestone 1: 10% of setup fee on contract signing — work starts on receipt
- Milestone 2: 40% of setup fee at design review
- Milestone 3: 50% of setup fee on final approval before go-live
- Monthly subscription: £99/month starting from launch date (optional but strongly recommended)
- Late payment: reminder at 7 days overdue, suspension of work at 14 days overdue
- All payments via Stripe

**Ownership and rights:**
- Client owns the final website and all their own content
- Wimbledon Smart retains all intellectual property in the plugin
- Wimbledon Smart retains the right to show the site in their portfolio unless the client objects in writing within 30 days of launch

**Responsibilities:**
- Client: provide content and assets within 5 working days, give timely feedback, pay on schedule
- Wimbledon Smart: design, build, configure, train, and provide ongoing support

**Data and compliance:**
- Client owns all their customer data
- Client is the data controller under UK GDPR for their customers' bookings and personal data
- On cancellation: Wimbledon Smart provides a full data export within 7 days, deactivates the plugin after 30 days, and permanently deletes all client data after 90 days

**Legal:**
- 30-day bug fix warranty after launch — any issues caused by the build will be fixed at no charge
- Limitation of liability capped at the total fees paid by the client
- UK law applies; disputes resolved in England and Wales

### After Signing

- Save signed contract PDF to Google Drive: `[Business Name]/Contracts/`
- Update Bonsai status to **Active Project**
- Move immediately to Step 2.2 — do not wait

## Step 2.2 — Raise Invoice 1 (10%)

### Amount

| Pricing Option | Setup Fee | Invoice 1 (10%) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard monthly | £995 | £99.50 |
| Standard annual | £995 | £99.50 |
| Introductory (first 2 clients) | £495 | £49.50 |

### How to Send

Raise the invoice through Bonsai, which generates a Stripe payment link. The client pays by card online — no bank transfers, no chasing payment by phone.

Set a due date of 5 working days. Add a note to the invoice:

> *"Once payment is confirmed I'll send everything you need to get started. The 5-working-day clock for asset delivery begins from the date of payment."*

### Work Does Not Begin Until Payment Is Confirmed

This is a hard rule. Do not start any work — staging environment, domain research, design sketches — until Invoice 1 is paid. Stripe sends you an instant notification on payment.

Once paid, move immediately to Step 2.3.

## Step 2.4 — Intake Questionnaire

Build this once in Bonsai or Google Forms. Send the same link to every client. It is the single source of truth for everything you need to build the site — do not start a build without a completed questionnaire.

### Section 1 — Business Details

- Legal business name and trading name (if different)
- Business address (for booking notifications and contact page)
- Phone number (for contact page and booking notifications)
- Email address for booking notifications (this is where all booking confirmations will be sent)
- Existing website URL (if any)
- Social media links — Facebook, Instagram, other
- VAT number (if registered)

### Section 2 — Branding

- Logo file upload (SVG or PNG preferred — if not available, describe what you have)
- Primary brand colour (hex code, or describe — e.g. "deep navy blue")
- Secondary brand colour (if any)
- Font preferences (if any — otherwise Wimbledon Smart will choose)
- Three websites whose design you admire — URLs and what you like about each
- Three words to describe the look and feel you want (e.g. clean and modern, warm and welcoming, professional and confident)
- Anything you definitely want to avoid (colours, styles, specific elements)

### Section 3 — Services

Repeat for each service (up to 15):

- Service name
- Brief description (1–2 sentences for the services page)
- Duration in minutes
- Price (£)
- Which staff members deliver this service
- Deposit required? (Yes/No — if yes: fixed amount £ or percentage %)
- Any booking notes for customers (e.g. "please arrive 10 minutes early", "consultation required before first booking")
- Should this service be bookable online, or enquiry only?

### Section 4 — Staff Members

Repeat for each staff member (up to 10):

- Full name and job title/role
- Working days (tick: Mon / Tue / Wed / Thu / Fri / Sat / Sun)
- Working hours (start and end time each day — or "varies")
- Lunch break or regular time blocks to exclude from bookings
- Buffer time needed between appointments (e.g. 15 minutes for room turnover)
- Google Calendar email address (for calendar sync — client must grant access once site is live)
- Profile photo (upload — optional but recommended)
- Short bio for team page (2–3 sentences — optional)

### Section 5 — Booking Preferences

- How far in advance can customers book? (e.g. up to 8 weeks)
- Minimum notice required for a new booking? (e.g. 24 hours, 48 hours)
- Can customers cancel online? If yes, minimum hours before appointment?
- Can customers reschedule online? If yes, minimum hours before appointment?
- Is a deposit forfeited on late cancellation? (Yes/No — if yes, how many hours is "late"?)
- Payment methods to offer: Stripe card payment / Pay on arrival / Both
- Should new customers be able to book immediately, or is an approval step required?

### Section 6 — Content

- About the business — 2–3 paragraphs or bullet points (informal is fine, will be edited and polished)
- Staff bios — one short paragraph per staff member if not already provided in Section 4
- Existing testimonials or Google reviews to feature on the site (paste text or share links)
- Photos — upload here or share via Google Drive (location shots, staff, treatments, before/after where relevant — smartphone photos are absolutely fine)
- Tagline or slogan (if you have one)
- Any specific wording, phrases, or tone requirements
- Any pages beyond the standard set (Home, About, Services, Booking, Contact)? If yes, describe what each page needs

### Section 7 — Technical

- Domain registrar (where your domain is registered — e.g. GoDaddy, 123-reg, Namecheap, Google Domains)
- Do you have access to the domain admin panel? (Yes/No — if no, who manages it?)
- Google Analytics account (if existing — GA4 tracking ID)
- Google Business Profile URL (if existing — paste the link from Google Maps)
- Any other booking or business tools currently in use that need to be considered

### Section 8 — Legal

- Do you have an existing Privacy Policy and/or Terms and Conditions? (If yes, upload or paste URL — if no, Wimbledon Smart will generate GDPR-compliant versions for you to review)
- Does your business process any sensitive customer data? (e.g. medical history for physiotherapists, health questionnaires for fitness coaches — this triggers additional GDPR steps)

## Step 2.5 — Asset Collection

### What You Need

By the end of the asset collection window (5 working days from Invoice 1 payment) you should have:

- [ ] Logo file — SVG or PNG, high resolution
- [ ] Brand colours — hex codes or clear description
- [ ] Photos — at least 5–10 usable images (quality varies — assess on receipt)
- [ ] Written content — rough notes at minimum for About page and service descriptions
- [ ] Completed questionnaire — all sections filled in

### Where Assets Are Stored

Everything goes into Google Drive: `[Business Name]/Brief & Assets/`

Organise on receipt:
```
Brief & Assets/
  ├── Logo/
  ├── Photos/
  ├── Content/         (written drafts, copy notes)
  └── Questionnaire/   (completed form export or PDF)
```

### Common Asset Issues and How to Handle Them

**No logo**
> *"No problem at all — this is very common. We have two options: I can build the site with placeholder branding and we swap in the logo when it's ready (adds a few days at the end), or I can recommend a designer who can turn one around quickly. Which works better for you?"*

Do not delay the entire build waiting for a logo. Build with placeholder branding and note it in Bonsai under Flags.

**Only smartphone photos**
This is fine and very common for your target clients. Smartphone photos taken in good light are perfectly usable. If they are very poor quality, address it honestly:

> *"The photos you've sent are a bit dark — if you can retake a few near a window in natural light, they'll come out much better on the site. I can show you what to aim for if helpful."*

**Missing or thin written content**
If the client has provided very little copy, you have two options:
- Edit and expand what they gave you (within scope — you are building a website, light copy editing is expected)
- If they expect you to write everything from scratch, have an honest conversation: *"Writing the full content from scratch is outside what the setup fee covers — I can do it as an add-on for [£X], or you can send me rough notes and I'll work from those."*

Set expectations clearly here. Scope creep on content is the most common cause of project delays.

**Assets arrive late**
If the 5-working-day window passes with no assets or an incomplete questionnaire, send one chase email:

> *"Hi [First Name], just checking in — I haven't received the questionnaire and assets yet. As a reminder, the 6-week timeline runs from the date I have everything. If you're having difficulty with any section, I'm happy to jump on a quick call and go through it together. [booking link]"*

If still nothing after a further 5 working days, call them directly. Note the delay in Bonsai — the timeline shifts accordingly and this is not Wimbledon Smart's responsibility.

## Step 2.6 — Brief Review and Sign-Off

### What to Do

Once the questionnaire is complete and assets are in the Drive folder:

1. **Review questionnaire completeness** — go through every section. Flag anything missing or ambiguous.
2. **Chase missing items once** — one short email listing exactly what is still needed. Be specific.
3. **Create the internal Project Brief** — a single document in Google Drive that summarises everything you need to build the site. This is your reference throughout Stage 3.
4. **Send build start confirmation** to the client.
